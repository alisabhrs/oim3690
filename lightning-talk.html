Lightning talk · News · Nov 2026

United just banned AI from booking your flights

The rule

AI can search, not book

Assistants can still show you United’s fares. They just can’t buy the ticket.

United’s reason

Your info at risk

An AI with your login, card and miles could make surprise purchases or changes.

Critics say

About sales too

On United’s own app it can sell you upgrades, bags and its credit card. An AI skips all that.

Sources: Skift, Nov 11, 2026 · Digital Trends · Business Traveller

The trade-off

Booking a flight isn’t like ordering on Amazon

Upside

It never sleeps

An AI can watch prices 24/7 and grab the cheapest fare faster than any human.

Downside

It needs everything

Your passport, airline login, credit card and miles: your most sensitive info.

Middle option

AI watches, you book

Let the AI track prices and alert you. You make the purchase yourself.
